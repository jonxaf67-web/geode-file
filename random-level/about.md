# Random Level

Find the button on the bottom row of the main menu.

**Random Level** – pick a difficulty range and level lengths, press **Roll!**, and get sent to a random online level that matches.

**Sightreading Practice** – generates a brand-new layout every time. Choose the difficulty (1–10), the length, and which gamemodes and speeds can appear, then press **Generate & Play**. Generated levels aren't saved; quitting returns you to the main menu.
